# Deep Dive: Are We on the Right Path? (2026-11-08)

## 1. Where we actually are

Ironclad A0, honest search, which means the agent never reads hidden RNG or the draw order.

| milestone | wins / 150 | avg floor |
|---|---|---|
| honest baseline (K=8, Phase 1) | 1 | 22.4 |
| native C++ search, K=64 (Phase 2; holdout 7 / 26.7) | 6 | 24.6 |
| + card-reward search (Phase 3) | 2 | 28.7 |
| + card search Act 1 only + rest search (holdout gate) | 2 (vs 7) | 29.3 (vs 26.7) |
| K=128 (holdout) | 5 (vs 7) | 26.7 |

**What these results show:**
- Average floor went up a lot: 22.4 → 29.3. **Wins stayed at about 4%**, and the last two levers lowered them.
- **Floor is the wrong target.** Runs that used to die in Act 1 now survive and die in Acts 2 and 3. That raises the floor without raising the win rate.
- We have been optimizing a proxy. On eval-150 at about 4% wins, a real +3-point win gain is not even detectable, because the 95% CI on 6/150 is 1.8–8.5%.

Research background:
- Determinization (our K-sample worlds) suffers from **strategy fusion**: each sampled world assumes a different, impossible plan. Information-set MCTS was built to fix that ([Cowling, Powley & Whitehouse 2012](https://eprints.whiterose.ac.uk/75048/1/CowlingPowleyWhitehouse2012.pdf)).
- That matches our finding that more samples stop helping past K=64.

## 3. What this means

1. **The honesty rule is the main reason this is hard.**
   - Both bots with strong win rates appear to search combat on the exact state.
   - Our own clairvoyant draft teacher reached 44/150, and honest K=8 reached 1/150.
   - Honest high win rates are clearly possible (humans, and LLMs at 98% A0), but they need real planning under uncertainty, not more brute force.
   - **This is Justin's call** (§6, decision 1). It changes every timeline below.
2. **Non-combat decisions are the cheapest big lever, and they must be learned against the run outcome.**
   - Jialeiv got +11 floors and 6% → 14% wins from a tiny network trained with REINFORCE in about 10 minutes.
   - destire's whole non-combat layer is one network.
   - Our hand heuristics plus short-horizon macro search optimize the next 6 floors, which is myopic, and that is why wins fell.
   - **Our Phase 3 plan already says "learned prior + value nets", but we went to search-for-macro first. Switch to learned non-combat policy trained on wins.**
3. **Combat: improve the rollout policy, not the sample count.**
   - K saturates at 64. The rollouts are played by varN, the old PPO net, so the search can't be better than "varN plus 1-ply correction".
   - The standard fix is policy iteration (expert iteration):
     - train a new rollout policy to imitate the honest K=64 search choices (we already have 403k labelled decisions);
     - search with it, then repeat.
   - Jialeiv's combat distillation failed because the teacher saw the future, which is exactly the Phase 1 diagnosis. Our teacher is honest, so its choices are learnable.
   - Our value-leaf failure fits the same pattern as their value-lookahead failure: a half-trained value net misleads search. Keep values out of the tree until the policy is strong.
4. **Measure wins with enough seeds.**
   - Gate on wins at ≥ 500 seeds. eval500 is running now.
   - Use floor only for diagnosis, plus intermediate win-like targets such as Act 1/2/3 boss kills per run.

## 4. Revised plan (Phase 3 onward)

| step | what | cost on the laptop | expected effect (literature-based guess) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 3.1 | eval500 baseline of mainline_h1 (running) | 3 h | win rate ± ~1.8 pts |
| 3.2 | **RL non-combat policy:** candidate-scorer MLP over all non-combat screens, REINFORCE / PPO on the run outcome (win + floor progress), with combat fixed to honest K=8 C++ (~12 s per seed); then evaluate with K=64 combat | 1–2 days (≈15k training games) | the largest expected gain: Jialeiv saw +11 floors and 2.3× wins over heuristics |
| 3.3 | **Combat policy iteration:** train a rollout policy (CE on argmax-Q) from the 403k honest K=64 labels, swap it in as the C++ rollout net, re-harvest, repeat 2–3× | 1 day per iteration | raises the K saturation point; this is the standard expert-iteration gain |
| 3.4 | alternate 3.2 and 3.3 (each improves the other's environment); gate on eval500 wins | ongoing | milestone 25% A0 |
| 3.5 | (if needed) information-set search for combat instead of determinized 1-ply, to fix strategy fusion | 3–5 days of engineering | helps on boss fights where planning matters |

## 5. Timeline estimates

These are wall-clock estimates on this laptop, assuming one researcher-agent and the current pace. **Confidence drops sharply after Phase 3.**

| milestone | honest rule kept | if combat may read the exact state |
|---|---|---|
| Phase 3: **25%** Ironclad A0 | 2–4 weeks | 1 week |
| Phase 3: **50%** A0 | +1–2 months | +2–3 weeks |
| Phase 3: **75–85%** A0 | +2–4 months (uncertain; needs strong planning) | +1 month |
| Phase 4: A10 70% / A15 55% / A20 40% | +3–6 months after 75% A0 (A20 honest 40% would beat today's best LLM, 20%) | +1–2 months (destire reached ~50% A20 Heart this way) |
| Phase 5: Silent (the sim has no Silent cards: ~75 cards, relics, events, differential tests vs the real game) | 2–4 months of engineering, can run in parallel | same |
| Phase 6: Defect, Watcher (orbs; stances) | 3–6 months of engineering | same |
| Phase 7: Act 4 / Heart, real-game validation, final table | 1–2 months | same |

**Overall:** 25% A0 honest within a month is realistic. All characters at high ascension is a 1–2 year project on one laptop, and the honest rule roughly doubles the RL timelines. Extra compute, such as one rented 64-core box at ~€60/month, would cut the RL phases by about 4×.

## 6. Decisions for Justin

1. **The honesty rule.**
   - Keep it, so we get a human-equivalent agent and the result is more meaningful (it can be compared to humans and LLMs).
   - Or allow exact-state combat search like the other bots, which is a much faster path to high win rates.
   - A middle option is to keep honest as the headline and run the exact-state variant as a separate track.
2. **Approve the plan pivot:** wins as the gate metric on 500 seeds, and the learned non-combat policy (3.2) and combat policy iteration (3.3) before more macro search.
3. **Compute:** stay on the laptop, or add a cheap cloud box for the RL phases.
